Explore Nardouw

Experience the Cederberg.

01

Bouldering

North Nardouw’s sandstone offers a varied mix of problems, from accessible warm-ups to steep, technical lines. Climb among weathered boulders, pause for the open views, and return to the cabin when the day winds down.

02

Nature walks

Wander through fynbos and rugged rock formations, or follow quiet roads between rooibos fields while taking in the surrounding Cederberg landscape.

03

Bike rides

Enjoy peaceful rides along quiet dirt roads winding through organic rooibos tea fields, surrounded by the open beauty of the Cederberg.

04

Rooibos tours

Explore the rooibos fields on a guided farm tour and learn the story of this uniquely Cederberg crop from plant to cup.

Make Nardouw your base.

Stay close to the landscape and explore at your own pace.
